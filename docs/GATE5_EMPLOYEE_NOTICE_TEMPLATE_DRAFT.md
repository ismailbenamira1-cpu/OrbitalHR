# OrbitalHR — Customer Employee Privacy Notice Template (DRAFT)
**Purpose:** Optional template customers can adapt for their own workforce; not a substitute for their applicable employee privacy notices, agreements or legal grounds. **Prepared:** 15 November 2026.

## Suggested employee-facing language
**Who is using the information:** [CUSTOMER LEGAL NAME], your [employer/other applicable relationship], is using a third-party mobility and compliance-screening tool called OrbitalHR, operated by [CURRENT ORBITALHR LEGAL OPERATOR NAME, AFTER VERIFICATION].

**Why:** To record planned or completed international work/travel arrangements, identify internal review priorities concerning immigration/work permission, employer tax presence, individual tax residence, social-security coverage and related payroll considerations, and obtain appropriate professional advice.

**What information:** We may include your name, work role, home and host jurisdiction, number of recorded travel/stay days, payroll arrangement, selected visa-extension status and social-security certificate status. We should not upload government numbers, passport scans, health records or unrelated sensitive information into unsupported fields.

**How screening works:** OrbitalHR applies simplified rules that generate workflow indicators and may provide optional third-party AI explanations. These are not legal findings or decisions. The employer/customer remains responsible for independent human review and appropriate local professional advice before consequential decisions.

**AI and service providers:** OrbitalHR uses Supabase and Cloudflare for software infrastructure and a configured external AI provider for optional generation. Structured AI prompts substitute saved employee names with aliases and omit record IDs, but employment/travel combinations and free-text questions may still identify individuals. Cross-border processing may occur; the customer must verify and appropriately describe relevant vendors and locations.

**Who may access information:** Only authenticated users authorized for the relevant OrbitalHR account under its current single-login model; the customer should not assume a shared multiuser company workspace exists. Cloud provider personnel may have limited access consistent with applicable agreements.

**Retention and requests:** The customer will retain records only as necessary for legitimate purposes under its own schedule and applicable law. The current OrbitalHR MVP permits deletion of individual saved roster rows, but it does not yet support automated company-level closure or a verified 30-day company-deletion service. For employee questions, contact [CUSTOMER PRIVACY CONTACT]. If applicable, privacy questions relating to OrbitalHR can be raised at ismailbenamira1@gmail.com.

**Accuracy, access and corrections:** Request corrections or access using [CUSTOMER PROCEDURE]. The customer will coordinate with OrbitalHR where necessary and subject to applicable rights and restrictions.

## Customer instructions before issuing
- Identify the actual employing entity, whether notice/consent/collective processes are required, and the worker jurisdictions.
- Do not claim AI inputs are anonymous or the vendor has zero retention.
- Do not suggest the tool grants visas, determines tax residency or clears employment decisions.
- Confirm retention, transfers, third-party contracts and incident support before allowing uploads.
- Have the customer review its final notice under applicable law; this is not an automatic legal permission to transfer employee data.
