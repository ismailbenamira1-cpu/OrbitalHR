# OrbitalHR — Acceptable Use and AI Screening Notice (DRAFT)

**Status:** Draft supplement to Terms and Privacy Notice. **Contact:** ismailbenamira1@gmail.com.

## Intended use
OrbitalHR provides preliminary internal workflow screening for international employee mobility. The platform helps flag topics for qualified review: immigration and work permission, corporate permanent establishment, individual tax residence, social-security treatment and payroll/EOR feasibility.

## Not legal clearance
Outputs are generated from user-recorded facts, simplified screening logic and optional AI explanations. Policy levels (including WATCH, CRITICAL and ESCALATE) are workflow priorities, **not legal findings or proof of a violation**. Low-risk/OK indicators do not establish work authorization or compliance. Tax residency is not itself a breach. Users must independently obtain appropriate professional guidance before consequential employment, immigration, tax or payroll decisions.

## Data entry and AI disclosure
Only input employment-related facts you are authorized to handle. Minimize detail and do not enter passport scans, government identifiers, health, financial-account, personal addresses, birth dates or unrelated sensitive narratives. When AI features are invoked, OrbitalHR transmits task-relevant screening details and user questions/history to a configured third-party AI provider. It substitutes known saved employee names with labels and excludes saved database IDs from its structured roster prompts, but **cannot guarantee personal identifiers will never be transmitted**, particularly in free-form text or combinations of information.

## Prohibited activity
Do not: access another account's data; circumvent sign-in, throttles or anti-abuse controls; instruct AI to disclose private or system information; upload materials you lack authority to share; scrape or resell customer data; use alerts for discriminatory profiling or unreviewed adverse employment decisions; impersonate an authority; or publish screening results as authoritative legal advice.

## Reporting concerns
To report privacy, access, accuracy, abuse or AI-output concerns, contact ismailbenamira1@gmail.com, providing only the necessary contextual details. Do not include sensitive employee documents in initial emails. The operator will review reports in accordance with an established incident and privacy-request process once finalized.

## MVP-specific limitations
Current data access is scoped to each signed-in **individual user's roster**, not a shared company workspace. Automated organization-wide closure, 30-day deletion execution, reviewed provider agreements and external legal sign-off are not yet complete. This document must not suggest that these controls are live.

**Deployment requirement:** show a concise notice near AI features and link this policy and the actual Privacy Notice after legal review; do not claim a complete data-minimization guarantee.
