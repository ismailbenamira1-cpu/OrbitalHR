## Acceptance fields required per cell

- Official primary-source URL and date checked
- Home nationality/citizenship/SS coverage and host visa route, employee work activities
- Days calculation period, prior visits, approved permission/expiry, entry and exit dates
- Applicable tax treaty and PE type, physical location, contract authority
- Adviser name, jurisdiction, license, written sign-off and expiry/re-review date

Source changes or conflicting interpretations must suspend automated compliance conclusions until re-approved.
